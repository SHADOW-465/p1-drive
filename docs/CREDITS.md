## Engine
Godot Engine 4.7.2 (MIT licence), https://godotengine.org

## Music
The radio plays the user's own files from a local folder. No music is stored in this repo.

## Trademarks
Car shapes are "inspired by" real vehicles. Real car names and designs belong to their owners. Personal use only; never publish.

## Local-only car models (assets_nc/, git-ignored)
`assets_nc/cars/mclaren_600lt/` holds a user-supplied Sketchfab "2019 McLaren 600LT" GLB for personal play. It is never committed or published; without it the game falls back to the P1. Add more cars by dropping a GLB under assets_nc/cars/ and adding one entry to `data/cars.json`.
